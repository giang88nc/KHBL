KIM HANH 2 - CAI HTTPS CHO PC LAN (KHBL - tiemvangkimhanh2)
=============================================================

1. Copy NGUYEN THU MUC "LAN_HTTPS_KIT" sang PC can dung (USB hoac chia se mang).
2. Chuot phai CAI_HTTPS_PC_LAN.bat -> Run as administrator (hoac chay thuong, se tu hoi quyen).
3. Bam Yes khi Windows hoi quyen quan tri.
4. Dong TOAN BO cua so Edge/Chrome/Firefox roi mo lai.
5. Vao: https://tiemvangkimhanh2:8100/banle/ban-hang/
   (go http://tiemvangkimhanh2:8100 cung duoc - tu nhay sang https)

Script lam gi:
 - Them dong "192.168.1.6  tiemvangkimhanh2" vao file hosts cua Windows.
 - Nap chung chi CA noi bo "Caddy Local Authority - 2026 ECC Root" (han 05/2036) vao Trusted Root.
   PC nao da cai kit HTTPS cua nen tang KIMHANH (cong 1276) thi CA da co san, script chi them hosts.
 - Mo trinh duyet toi trang ban hang.

Luu y:
 - Chi cai chung chi do quan tri vien Kim Hanh cung cap.
 - Neu IP may chu doi (khong con 192.168.1.6) -> chay lai file .bat sau khi quan tri vien sua IP trong script.
 - Chi https moi mo duoc camera (chup anh khach / CCCD).
